# Product Promo Template Storyboard

## Format

- Duration: 12 seconds
- Canvas: 1920×1080
- Language: Chinese
- Motion energy: medium, with one stronger CTA transition

## Scenes

### 01 — Problem / 0.0–4.2s

- Fragmented production queue enters from the right.
- Headline establishes the workflow problem.
- Slow grid drift keeps the frame active.

### 02 — Feature / 3.8–9.0s

- A directional push overlaps scene 01 by 0.4 seconds.
- Five pipeline stages reveal in order.
- Progress rails visualize deterministic completion.

### 03 — CTA / 8.6–12.0s

- A scale-through transition marks the payoff.
- Brand lockup and CTA enter over a restrained orbital motif.

## Caption timing

Captions are root-level clips timed to voice beats. Scene timing may change without changing
caption boundaries; narration timing remains the source of truth.
