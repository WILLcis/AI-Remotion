## Video direction

Palette uses the frame spec's ink `#05070b` as the dark field, cream `#f4f3ee` for readable type, violet `#8b7cff` for agent identity, green `#4ade80` for one active-state accent, and tinted neutrals for structure. Display copy is serif; UI labels and captions are sans; IDs are mono. Motion uses deterministic index-derived layouts, smooth long-tail settling, VO-paced sequential reveals, and velocity-matched seams. Frame 1 is the high-density expansion; Frame 2 is the controlled convergence and mechanism proof; Frame 3 deliberately resolves into a calm held brand read. Keep all load-bearing content above the bottom caption band. Never front-load then freeze, never float every element independently, never use bouncy overshoot, neon gradients, fake product screenshots, customer metrics, or unsupported scale claims.
