## Authored visual candidates

- `leader-node` — one restrained human/leader identity node; abstract and non-photographic.
- `agent-node-field` — a deterministic field of coding-agent worker nodes with status and task-role labels; conveys coordination scale without claiming a numeric operating ratio.
- `task-stream` — issue cards with safe synthetic IDs such as `ACME-42`; statuses use `queued`, `working`, `review`, and `done`.
- `unified-board` — a conceptual control-plane board, explicitly stylized rather than presented as a product screenshot.
- `deepdog-wordmark` — lowercase text wordmark only; no invented logo mark.
- `control-lines` — routing paths that visibly connect leader, agent nodes, task cards, and the unified board.

All visual assets will be authored as HTML/CSS/SVG motion graphics. No customer metrics, customer identities, endorsements, or unsupported product capabilities may appear.
