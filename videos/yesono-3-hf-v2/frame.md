# YesONO 3.0 visual system

Premium institutional B2B finance, built from calm authority rather than spectacle.

## Palette roles

- Ink `#0A101C` is the composition canvas; letterbox `#05080F` owns deep transitions and caption fades.
- Panels use `#131C2E` and `#0F1727`, separated by cool low-opacity rules rather than heavy shadows.
- Brass `#D4B36A` marks operator ownership, key commercial outcomes, and decisive statements.
- Jade `#6FC2A0` marks safety, verification, completion, and controlled settlement.
- Harbor `#6FA3DC` marks platform infrastructure, data, market flow, and liquidity.
- Primary text is `#E8EEF7`; explanatory text is `#9FB0C7`; metadata is `#6B7C95`.

## Typography

- Serif display: institutional headlines, decisive claims, and closing lines. Use 72–118px in 16:9.
- Sans body: operational explanations and captions. Use 28–40px.
- Mono metadata: scene numbering, market labels, ledger ids, state tags, and timing rails. Use 18–24px with generous tracking.
- Never use gradient text, playful geometric display fonts, or web-scale copy.

## Composition

- Use asymmetric editorial framing, layered depth, full-width process strips, and structural rules.
- Preserve the lower 17% caption keep-out.
- Market diagrams must communicate real roles: orders, risk, matching, ledger, settlement, tenant boundaries, liquidity, or revenue.
- Use deterministic SVG paths, measured line systems, index-seeded particles, order-book bars, and ledger rows.

## Motion

- Smooth long-tail settles; decisive fast attacks; no bounce or elastic overshoot.
- Reveal each claim on its spoken cue across the full scene. Hold resolved frames still.
- Motion can be rich, but systems move as coordinated flows rather than independent floating widgets.
- Favor line draws, ordered pulses, focus pulls, velocity-matched seams, depth assembly, restrained particles, and data fills.

## Avoid

- Neon cyberpunk, purple-blue AI gradients, holographic HUD clutter, floating bokeh, fake browser chrome.
- Generic six-card dashboards, tiny interface text, decorative claims, lazy breathing, endless loops, or unseeded randomness.
